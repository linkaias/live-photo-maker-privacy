English

Your chosen videos are processed locally in your browser. The extension does not upload your videos or generated files to the developer's servers.

What the extension handles

When you choose a video, the extension reads it on your device to preview it and generate a paired JPEG image and MOV video, which you may download individually or as a ZIP. Your video may include personal or sensitive content. Processing creates new files and does not change the original video.

Local settings and retention

The extension stores your interface language, editing preferences and an encoder-loading indicator in Chrome's local extension storage. Video data and generated results are used during the active session; downloaded files remain on your computer until you delete them. You can remove extension settings by uninstalling the extension and delete downloaded files using your operating system. Browser or operating-system caches and backups are managed separately.

Sharing and third parties

The extension does not send your selected videos, generated files or settings to the developer and does not include analytics or advertising services. If you choose to share or sync downloaded files using another service, that service's privacy practices apply. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process routine page-access data under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

The extension uses Chrome's side panel to provide its interface and local storage to remember your settings. It does not request access to websites.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the extension changes and will update the date above. For privacy questions, contact linkaiaslinkai@gmail.com.

简体中文

你选择的视频只在本机浏览器中处理。插件不会将视频或生成的文件上传到开发者服务器。

插件处理的信息

选择视频后，插件会在设备上读取视频用于预览，生成配对的 JPEG 图片和 MOV 视频；你可以分别下载，也可以下载 ZIP 压缩包。视频可能包含个人或敏感内容。处理会生成新文件，不会修改原视频。

本地设置与保存时间

插件在 Chrome 的本地扩展存储中保存界面语言、编辑偏好和编码器加载状态。视频数据和生成结果在当前使用过程中处理；下载的文件会保留在电脑上，直到你自行删除。卸载插件可移除插件设置；下载的文件需要通过操作系统自行删除。浏览器或操作系统的缓存和备份由其各自管理。

共享和第三方

插件不会将你选择的视频、生成的文件或设置发送给开发者，也没有集成统计分析或广告服务。如果你主动使用其他服务分享或同步下载的文件，将适用该服务的隐私政策。本政策页面托管于 GitHub Pages；GitHub 可能根据其隐私政策处理常规页面访问数据。

权限

插件使用 Chrome 侧边栏展示操作界面，使用本地存储记住设置。插件不申请访问网站的权限。

政策变更与联系

插件发生变化时，我们可能更新本政策并修改上方日期。如有隐私问题，请联系 linkaiaslinkai@gmail.com。
